Push Day

Shoulders · Chest · Triceps  ·  Tap any exercise to see how to do it

How to do it

Sit on a chair or stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward, elbows bent at 90°. Press both dumbbells straight up overhead until your arms are nearly straight — don't lock out. Slowly lower back to shoulder height. That's one rep.

Step by step
  • 1
    Start seated or standing, dumbbells at shoulder height
  • 2
    Elbows at 90°, palms facing forward
  • 3
    Press straight up until arms are nearly straight
  • 4
    Lower slowly back to start
💡 Form tip: Last 2 reps should be a real grind.

Pull Day

Back · Biceps  ·  Tap any exercise to see how to do it

How to do it

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, a dumbbell in each hand. Push your hips back and hinge forward until your torso is roughly 45° to the floor — like you're about to pick something up off a low table. Let the dumbbells hang straight down. Pull both dumbbells up toward your hips, squeezing your shoulder blades together at the top. Lower slowly.

Step by step
  • 1
    Stand feet shoulder-width, dumbbells in hand
  • 2
    Hinge forward at hips ~45°, back flat
  • 3
    Let dumbbells hang straight down
  • 4
    Pull up toward your hips, squeeze shoulder blades
  • 5
    Lower slowly and repeat
💡 Form tip: Pull toward your hip, not your chest — that keeps the back muscles doing the work.

How to do it

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging at your sides with palms facing forward. Keeping your upper arms completely still against your sides, curl both dumbbells up toward your shoulders by bending at the elbow. Squeeze your bicep at the top, then lower slowly all the way back down.

Step by step
  • 1
    Stand tall, dumbbells at sides, palms forward
  • 2
    Pin your upper arms against your sides — they don't move
  • 3
    Curl both dumbbells up to your shoulders
  • 4
    Squeeze at the top, then lower all the way down
💡 Form tip: Don't swing your back — if you have to rock to lift it, the weight is too heavy.

How to do it

Same starting position as a regular curl, but rotate your wrists so your palms face each other — thumbs pointing up, like you're holding hammers. Keeping upper arms still, curl both dumbbells up to your shoulders. Lower slowly. The neutral grip shifts work to the outer bicep and forearms, giving your arms that fuller look.

Step by step

  • 2
    Upper arms stay pinned to your sides
  • 3
    Curl up to shoulder height
  • 4
    Lower slowly all the way down
💡 Form tip: Same motion as a regular curl, just with thumbs up. Hits a different part of the bicep.
